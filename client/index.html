How to play

Push other pigs into the pit in the middle. Every pig you hook or shove in counts as your elimination. The first to the target wins.

  1. Hook from the rim. Stand at the edge, aim across the pit and press Shift. Your hook pulls the pig to just in front of you, so if you're at the rim, it drops right into the hole.
  2. Flick it in. Right after your hook lands, turn quickly. The pig gets dragged toward where you're facing, which helps when it's beside you instead of across the pit.
  3. Shove up close. V or mouse button 4 swipes your hook and knocks a nearby pig back. Push pigs standing at the rim over the edge.
  4. Deflect hooks. Swing your shove into an incoming hook just before it reaches you. It bounces back and the thrower is stunned. Miss, and you're stuck mid-swing.
  5. Breathe out of the pit. Got pulled in? Press E fast. Your breathing mask launches you upward, and you can steer back over the rim. It has a 7-second cooldown.

Move with WASD, jump with Space, hold Tab for scores, Esc to pause.
